## Two programming examples

| Source | What the accessible material prescribes | Assessment for this hybrid app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The BEST Kettlebell Workout For Anyone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zGxnnKrvDvY) | A half-hour repeatable circuit combines a hinge, unilateral overhead and squat work, and submaximal push-ups. Track quality rounds before increasing resistance; the description proposes three weekly sessions with recovery between them. | Useful density/progression example. Press plus push-up creates shared pressing fatigue; count both. Preserve a rowing/pulling slot in a three-day full-body program. Do not substitute this circuit for every strength anchor. |
| [One Kettlebell = An Entire Gym](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tmX3UZEQdoI) | Twenty-minute EMOM rotating snatch, sustainable push-up work, thruster and unilateral row, with load increased after control and capacity improve. | Useful single-bell conditioning template with pulling included. Thruster and push-up share pressing work; snatch adds overhead/grip demand. Density does not imply complete muscular recovery between minutes. |

The [public Foundation sample](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/kettlebell-workouts) separately accounts for warm-up, circuit and mobility time. Its wider Foundation schedule is not identical to the YouTube video's proposed weekly cadence; do not flatten them into one claimed protocol.

## Eight movement lessons

| Lesson | Useful takeaway | App implication / judgement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Swing](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/kettlebell-swing) | Hip-driven ballistic work, with arms controlling the bell; technique precedes dense volume. | Retain crisp power reps and a stop-before-technique-breakdown cue. Account for posterior-chain and grip fatigue. |
| [Clean](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/kettlebell-clean) | Close bell path and controlled transition into the rack. | Useful preparation for rack work. It is not a curl; repeated cleans still contribute hinge/grip work. |
| [Press](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/kettlebell-press) | Stable rack, trunk control and a controlled overhead finish. | Distinguish strict press from push press. The tutorial permits leg assistance; do not silently treat assisted reps as proof of improved strict-press strength. |
| [Squat](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/kettlebell-squat) | Comfortable rack/goblet setup, controlled depth and stable foot/knee position. | Keep single/double/goblet loading definitions explicit. Do not treat all variations as identical absolute strength tests. |
| [Lunge](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/kettlebell-lunge) | Unilateral rack work and balance against an offset load. | Good match for the retained unilateral KB day. Keep per-leg doses explicit and count trunk/leg overlap. |
| [Snatch](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/kettlebell-snatch) | Develop swing/clean/high-pull skill before high-volume overhead ballistics. | Technique/density option, not a compulsory heavy finisher after exhausted pressing. Hand/wrist tolerance is not inferred from load history. |
| [Thruster](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/kettlebell-thruster) | Continuous lower-body drive into an overhead finish. | Count leg and pressing demands together. Conditioning progression must not be used as strict-press progression. |
| [Double bells](https://www.kettlebellbody.app/learn/double-kettlebell-exercises) | Familiar movement patterns under greater total load. | Fits the matched heavier adjustable pair. Keep per-bell weights explicit and distinguish total load from single-bell load. |

## What changes now

The app's KB research drawers include the two programming examples and movement-matched links to these eight lessons, labelled **practitioner programming/technique**, alongside existing scientific sources. These are primary sources for the coach's methods, not comparative scientific proof or a guarantee of outcomes. Commercial slogans about replacing running, reversing age or outperforming an hour in a gym are not model inputs.

The established heavy/power/volume KB program remains intact in this release. The user asked for broader research inclusion, not an unreviewed wholesale replacement. The useful additions to the research basis are repeatable density blocks, skill progression, clear single/double load definitions, and explicit shared fatigue in complexes. Optional future templates should retain pulling, respect technique, use the bell dictated by the weakest movement, and progress one variable at a time.

For Will's setup, the 40 lb bell provides convenient lighter single-bell changes; the two heavier adjustable bells suit matched strength work or a single load held through a complex. Avoid prescribing rapid internal plate changes inside timed circuits. Keep the existing minimum-exact-plate-count and ordered-switching rules for between-block changes. Do not import the coach's demonstrated 32 kg load as a universal target.
