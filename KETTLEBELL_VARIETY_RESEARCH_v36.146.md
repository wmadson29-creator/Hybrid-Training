These are movement-pattern counts, not equal hypertrophy sets for every involved muscle. Swing rounds are not interchangeable with resistance sets. ## Hardware, cues, and progression

- With one bell, B's Gorilla Row maps to Single-Arm Row, and C's Double Reverse Lunge maps to Single Rack Reverse Lunge. C's strict press already uses one bell. Reps retain `/side` or `/leg`; loads follow the actual replacement and available hardware.
- C's strict-press cue says to clean once into the rack, press without leg drive, then change sides. It does not copy the clean-before-each-rep instruction from Double Clean & Press.
- Reverse-lunge cues specify controlled backward steps, each-leg counting, and completing both legs before the set rest. Gorilla-row cues specify a stable hinge, alternating arms, each-arm counting, and bells supported on the floor between pulls.
- Full-session progression continues to require comparable completed results in the same KB slot and exact exercise. Two comfortable comparable exposures earn the next rep step; reaching the top of the range earns an available load increase and a reset to the low end. Swings earn rounds before a heavier bell.
- Old C clean/press or front-squat results and old B Single-Arm Row loads cannot directly set the new movements' absolute loads. New movements use their own history and hardware-aware starting targets. Short add-ons do not earn full-session progression; completed full KB secondaries can.
- Generated presets and rows carry recipe version 146. Saved custom routines keep their own identity and prescription. Completed logs, active/paused work, drafts, recovery, and measurements are not rewritten.
