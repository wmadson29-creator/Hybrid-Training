RESEARCH / SYSTEMS AUDIT
Added RECOVERY_INTERACTIONS_RESEARCH_v36_96.md. The audit reinforces the existing multidimensional readiness approach and documents additional relationships that should stay contextual rather than become hardcoded prescription rules: high-speed running vs easy aerobic volume, endurance-modality interference uncertainty, active recovery vs actual restored performance, cold-water immersion tradeoffs, heat/sauna context, stretching/mobility context, and subjective/objective recovery integration.
No training coefficient was changed solely because a recovery intervention or observational signal has literature support.

LIMITATIONS
- Browser QA was performed in headless Chromium. This is not a physical Samsung/Android touch test.
- A final installed-PWA sanity check on the user’s phone remains appropriate after updating, especially for OS-specific viewport, keyboard, and service-worker behavior.
- Live third-party exercise-media availability depends on internet access and is outside the core offline app QA.
