# Hybrid Training v36.115

## Changes

- Landmine Press and Landmine Row now have unmistakable **single-loaded-end** plate logic in Log Workout. Entered weight is total implement weight: 45-lb bar + plates on the loaded end.
- Landmine cards explicitly label the field **Total weight (45 bar + loaded end)**, and name-based fallback detection protects custom landmine exercise names from the normal two-sided barbell helper.
- During **Barbell Strength Only**, automatic short complementary secondaries will not select kettlebells. Manual KB workouts are still available.
- A short kettlebell secondary is no longer mislabeled as **KB Heavy**; in phases where it is allowed, it remains a small guided secondary rather than masquerading as a full Heavy session.
- Automatic full second-workout recommendations remain limited to Conditioning, Generic Gym, Calisthenics, and Strength-Endurance. KB Heavy/Power/Volume are manual choices there.

No primary recommendation scoring changes were made.
