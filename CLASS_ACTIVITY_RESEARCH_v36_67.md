# Class & Activity Expansion Research — Hybrid Training v36.67

## What changed

v36.67 keeps **Classes** and **Activities** as separate navigation destinations while using one physiological engine underneath them. The distinction is organizational, not physiological: a hard independent activity can still carry more fatigue than a class, and a restorative class can carry almost none.

The catalog now includes additional boutique/studio classes and a much broader activity list: golf, hiking/backpacking, trail running, paddlesports, surfing, beach volleyball, table tennis, ultimate, softball/baseball, skating, skiing/snowboarding, snowshoeing, horseback riding, and others.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Direct peer-reviewed physiology studies/systematic reviews.
2. 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities measured MET entries.
3. Close physiological-family analogue when the named boutique format does not have direct literature.
4. Lower-confidence prior, with the user's actual HR/RPE/duration/active-time metrics allowed to dominate.

The Compendium is an intensity classification tool, **not** a precise individualized calorie predictor.

## Key additions

### Pilates Jumpboard / Cardio Reformer
The user's Workshop Pilates example describes a 50-minute Reformer class that adds low-impact jumpboard intervals specifically to raise heart rate/endurance while retaining Pilates strength/control. Direct Pilates evidence supports ordinary Pilates as generally light-to-moderate metabolic work, with apparatus/Reformer tending above Mat. Jumpboard is therefore modeled above ordinary Reformer for aerobic/anaerobic demand, but with much less landing impact than upright plyometrics.

### Boutique HIFT / mixed studio
GRIT/F45-style functional circuits use the HIFT evidence base: high-density sessions can produce high HR, lactate and RPE, but the exact WOD/station structure strongly affects dose. OrangeTheory-/Barry's-style formats are modeled from their actual components (treadmill/row/floor resistance), not from a marketing-name calorie assumption.

### BodyCombat / athletic aerobics
Direct group-fitness work found BodyCombat, step and indoor cycling produced high energy expenditure, while resistance-focused PUMP was lower in average HR/energy expenditure. BodyCombat therefore receives substantial aerobic/anaerobic credit without the contact/impact profile of live sparring.

### Outdoor / sport activities
The 2024 Compendium directly supports:
- Ultimate Frisbee: 8.0 MET
- Golf walking/carrying: 4.3 MET; walking/pulling: 4.5; cart: 3.5
- Beach volleyball: 8.0
- Table tennis: 4.0
- Backpacking: about 7.0–7.8
- Kayaking: ~5.0 moderate to 13.5 competition
- SUP: ~2.8–11 depending on stroke rate
- Recreational/fast inline skating: ~7.5–15.5
- Downhill skiing/snowboarding: ~4.3–8 active time
- Cross-country skiing: ~6.8–16
- Snowshoeing: ~5.3–10

These are used as population-level intensity anchors; the app still uses entered duration, active time, HR, RPE, distance, elevation and conditions to personalize the session.
