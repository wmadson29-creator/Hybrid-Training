LEGACY REGRESSION HARNESS NOTES
The older deep QA harness was re-run far enough to identify several obsolete expectations rather than app defects:
- moderate local fatigue was previously expected to always emit a warning; current research-calibrated logic intentionally warns only at substantial overlap;
- the old harness expected an obsolete weighted-pull-up Block 2 sequence;
- the old harness expected two easy endurance exposures to automatically progress duration, while the current model deliberately requires stronger repeated evidence.
These old assertions were not used to force the model backward.

LIMITATIONS
- Browser QA was performed in headless Chromium and is not a physical Samsung/Android installed-PWA touch test.
- A final installed-PWA sanity check on the user’s phone remains appropriate for OS-specific viewport, keyboard, and service-worker behavior.
- Live third-party exercise-media availability depends on internet access and is outside the core offline app QA.
