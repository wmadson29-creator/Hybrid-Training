# Hybrid Training v36.150 — recovery freshness and effort evidence

Implements all four improvements accepted after reviewing export (24). Baseline: v36.149. Prepared October 7, 2026, America/Phoenix.

- **Recovery across midnight:** Recommendations can use yesterday's timestamped observations while today has no check-in. The source date and age appear in Recovery context. Today's form stays blank, steps are not copied, and nothing is written into a new recovery record. A new check-in supersedes the context. Body fatigue/soreness expire after 36 hours; alertness/current BioCharge/stress after 12 hours. Prior sleep, waking BioCharge, resting HR and HRV expire at 06:00 or 24 hours, whichever comes first. Future and historical previews do not borrow today's observations. These are bounded freshness policies, not physiological cutoffs. Recommendation evidence changes when eligible fields expire.
- **Class effort priors:** Use the activity library's class-specific RPE range before generic conditioning defaults. F45-style Functional Circuit starts from its 7–9 prior; Pilates and Lagree retain their own priors. Residual calibration is separated by class family. Old hard-class predictions at 5 or below are re-baselined for learning without overwriting saved predictions or actual effort. Class focus still reaches local muscle fatigue and bounded development; it never fabricates lift results.
- **Recent exercise effort:** Two completed, recent, comparable results that consistently contradict the prediction can adjust the estimate by at most 1.5 RPE. Comparability requires the same exercise, session/KB slot, sets, reps and load. Confident RPE takes priority over clean reps remaining, then exercise effort bands. One workout earns one observation; projected work and short add-ons cannot earn this adjustment. Unknown, incomplete, skipped or mismatched evidence cannot establish the required pair. Confidence is capped at 0.68 for estimates or 0.78 for confident direct RPE. General workout experience remains independent.
- **Separate pull-up review:** Repeated very hard comparable pull-up results appear in target evidence and the existing block-end review. The current six-week Barbell wave, dedicated grip/load schedule, training maxes and review choices stay authoritative. This adds no automatic within-workout load changes. Export (24) has no actual grip field, so the evidence is exercise/dose-specific rather than a proven grip-specific plateau.
- **Durable barbell explanations:** Every new programmed Barbell target carries its block, prescription week and dose explanation. Main lifts identify training max, wave percentage and 5 lb rounding; pull-ups identify their separate scheduled grip/load; abs retain the existing slow-progression explanation. Drafts, quick completion, History/editing and JSON/CSV export/import preserve the recorded reason. Missing reasons on old completed records remain unknown.
- Advance app/cache/manifest to **36.150**, recommendation model stamp to **16**. Add `npm run test:freshness` to `test:all`. The KB preset recipe remains **147**.

The latest export has 199 workout rows, 42 recovery dates, eight bodyweight measurements and no completed KB sessions. It supports repairing class predictions and repeated pull-up effort estimates. It does not establish a global deload, blanket training-max increase or a different KB recipe. Actual records remain intact; the existing derived personal calibration fields may refresh.
