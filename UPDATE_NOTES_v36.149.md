# Hybrid Training v36.149 — target evidence and exercise feedback

Implements the user's selected improvements 1, 3, 4 and 5 from the October 5 recommendations.

- **Target explanations:** Today and resistance Log cards have a compact Why this target drawer with the prescribed dose, decision reason and latest three actual direct results. KB evidence stays specific to the exercise and slot. Projected, skipped and missed work never becomes displayed completed evidence. Barbell targets identify their programmed wave. Edited entries retain the generated prescription separately. New saves preserve the target and reason in History, editing, JSON/CSV export and import; older records identify missing decision reasons honestly.
- **Class intensity:** Selected muscles or movements can optionally be marked Light, Moderate or Hard. The model consumes those selections in local muscle attribution, recent fatigue and bounded development estimates. Default/Moderate retains the prior interpretation. Unchecking a focus removes its intensity. Duration, total conditioning load and direct lifting results are not invented from these choices. Intensity survives drafts, active-workout workspace snapshots, History edits and export/import.
- **Clearer exercise effort:** The optional Reps left / RPE disclosure accepts 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5+ more clean reps on the last working set. A selection can fill an otherwise missing/Not sure effort label. Confident RPE takes priority, followed by reps left, followed by the existing rough-RPE/effort interpretation. Raw entries remain separate; no numeric RPE is fabricated in saved data. Reps left supplies bounded model-calibration evidence. Swings, other explosive-only work, carries and timed holds do not use it. Substitution clears this exercise-specific input. Overall experience remains general feedback.
- **KB calibration:** Automatic starting bells are explicitly provisional until three completed exact-exercise/slot exposures with effort exist. A clearly easy completed baseline can try one available bell heavier; excessive strain/poor completion can try one lighter. Unknown effort repeats the bell. Recovery holds increases, and explosive work needs explicit crispness before an early increase. Existing earned two-exposure progression remains available during calibration. Forecasts never graduate it, duplicate rows count once, and short add-ons cannot earn it. Manual loads retain authority. The A/B/C recipe remains 147.
- **Draft integrity:** Preserve original planned exercise, dose, rest and expected effort when restoring a draft, alongside the newly recorded target reason. A class draft no longer inherits a regenerated default class as its original plan.
- Advance app/cache/manifest to **36.149** and recommendation model stamp to **15**. Make updater test fixtures derive current/next versions, and add `npm run test:feedback` to the complete QA command.

The 3-exposure calibration threshold, RIR uncertainty bands and local intensity factors (0.60/1.00/1.25) are bounded app policies, not validated physiological conversions. Reps remaining is an effort estimate; it does not establish an optimal prescription for every exercise. General research grounding includes [Refalo et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38393985/); that study does not validate this exact KB routine or these app coefficients.

Validation and install details are in `QA_REPORT_v36.149.md` and the maintained `RELEASE_HISTORY_AND_CHAT_HANDOFF.md`. The cumulative update ZIP retains all v36.140–148 changes. No repository upload or deployment was performed during preparation.
